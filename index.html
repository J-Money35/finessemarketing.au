Finesse Marketing Reporting

Finesse Marketing Reporting is our in-house software. It connects to the Google Ads API to build campaigns, apply approved changes and prepare reports for the Google Ads accounts our clients have given us access to. It is used only by Finesse Marketing staff, only on those accounts, and only for the work each client has engaged us to do. See our privacy policy for how that data is handled.
