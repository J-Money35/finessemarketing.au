Privacy policy

Finesse Marketing, Toowoomba QLD. Effective 24 November 2026.

The short version

  • Finesse Marketing is me, John Hewitt. I'm the only person who handles your information.
  • This website has no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising tags.
  • If you email me, I use what you send only to reply and to do the work you ask about.
  • For clients, I use the information needed to run your marketing, report to you and invoice you. I never sell it or mix one client's information with another's.
  • Some of the services I use store information overseas, mainly in the United States.
  • You can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or complain, at john@finessemarketing.au.

Who I am

Finesse Marketing is a trading name of J-Money Productions Pty Ltd, ABN 87 641 803 278, 5 McCallum Court, Middle Ridge QLD 4350. I follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What I collect

  • When you contact me: your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and whatever you choose to tell me.
  • When your business works with me: the names and contact details of the people I deal with, and the business details I need to do and invoice the work.
  • Inside your marketing accounts: when a client gives me access to their advertising, analytics, social media or business listing accounts, or their website, I can see what those accounts hold. That is mostly totals and campaign figures, but it can include enquiry details or customer lists. I use it only for that client's work.

I don't ask for sensitive information such as health details. If a client's work involves it, I handle it only as that client directs and keep it to what the job needs.

This website

This website has no contact form and sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking tags. Like any website host, the company that hosts it may record technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, to keep its service secure.

How I use information

  • To reply to you and to quote, deliver, report on and invoice the work you ask for.
  • To run and improve your campaigns, websites and tracking, when you are a client.
  • To keep the records the law requires, such as tax records.

I don't sell or rent personal information, I don't use one client's information for another, and I don't use it to build advertising profiles of you.

Services I use, including AI

I run my business on a small number of established online services, and information I work on passes through them:

  • Business email, calendar, file storage and accounting software.
  • The advertising, analytics and social media platforms your marketing runs on.
  • AI assistants from major providers, which help me draft, check and organise work. When I use them, the information I'm working on is sent to the provider to be processed under its privacy terms. I give them only what the task needs.

No decision about you is made by a computer alone; I review the work.

Google Ads data and my campaign tools

My campaign software connects to the Google Ads API using a Google account that clients have given access to their Google Ads accounts, directly or through my manager account. Through it I read campaign settings and performance data, and I create or change campaigns, keywords, ads, audiences and budgets when the client has approved that work.

  • I access only the Google Ads accounts my clients have given me access to, and only for the work they have engaged me to do.
  • I use the data to manage those accounts and report results to that client. I don't sell it, rent it, use it for advertising profiles, or combine one client's data with another's.
  • The tools are used only by me, John Hewitt. Access credentials are kept in protected credential storage, not in files or code.
  • Report data is stored in my business cloud storage and on my work computer, kept while I work with the client and as needed for my records afterwards. A client can ask me to delete it when the engagement ends, except where I must keep records by law.
  • My use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Who I share information with

  • The client it belongs to.
  • The platforms needed to do the work, such as the advertising platforms your campaigns run on.
  • The services I use to run the business, listed above.
  • Anyone the law requires me to tell.

I don't share it with anyone else without your agreement.

Information stored overseas

Several of the services I use store and process information outside Australia, mainly in the United States, and in other countries where they run data centres. I choose established providers with published privacy and security commitments.

Keeping it safe and how long I keep it

Only I have access. I keep credentials in protected storage, not in files or code, and I use the security settings each service offers. I keep information while I'm working with you, and business records for as long as the law requires (tax records for five years), then delete or securely archive them.

Your choices, access and complaints

You can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or raise a concern, by emailing john@finessemarketing.au. I'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with my answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes

I'll update this page when my practices change, and the date at the top shows the last change.
